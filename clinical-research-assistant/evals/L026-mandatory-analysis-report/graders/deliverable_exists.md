Per lesson L026, every CRA analysis must end with a date-stamped, structured
markdown deliverable named `analysis_report_<short-question-slug>_YYYY-MM-DD.md`
under the project's `Analyses/` or `Reports/` folder. A file matching that
glob should exist under the workspace by the end of the run.
